# Shopee Logistics Performance: Data Analytics Challenge

## About project

- Shopee is the leading e-commerce platform in Southeast Asia and Taiwan. It is a platform tailored for the region, providing customers with an easy, secure and fast online shopping experience through strong payment and logistical support.

- Shopee is now in partnership with 5 different Third-Party Logistics (3PLs) namely A, B, C, D, and E, also operating in 6 different routes: Intra city, Intra region, Intra region city, Cross region, Cross region city, Special same region.

- Data is from SCMISSION 2021 - ROUND 2 - PHASE 2.2

### Business context

- In 2020, each route shares different percentage of orders volume. For example, “Intra city” counts for 40% of total order volume, but also has the highest % average late rate, 15%.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a3cc9bc-1f6a-4ac9-a2f5-daa9538e81f3)

### Business challenge

- In 2020, Shopee maintains its current volume of 100 millions orders per month, and expects to increase this volume to 200 millions orders in 2021.

- In 2020, the average percentage of late delivery per month is around 20%. In the next year, Shopee focuses on reducing this rate to only 10%. 

***Question: HOW TO REACH TARGETED VOLUME AND REDUCE THE AVG. % OF LATE DELIVERY TO 10%?***
